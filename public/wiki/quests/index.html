Rules (paraphrased)

  • Quest points are awarded only if you finish the entire quest yourself, in order, without logging off mid-quest. Markers reset on logout or death; reboot breaks quests too.
  • Sharing quest information with other players is not allowed (including paid help or cross-guild spell-ups for quest fights).
  • Do not be hidden/invis when giving items to quest NPCs — they will not see you and keep the item.
  • Quest points grant an experience bonus on kills.
Quest progress is fragile: logging out, dying, or a reboot can reset markers. Plan uninterrupted time before starting a chain.
