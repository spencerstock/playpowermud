Drow artifact requirements

The note says you need 100,000 experience to attempt this, and the reward is a one-time random artifact. A drow asks you to lay these items out and then receive the prize.

  • The scale from a Two-headed Dragon
  • The scale from a Shadow Dragon
  • An Undamaged scale from a Gold Dragon
  • An Undamaged scale from a Blue Dragon
  • The scale from an Undead Dragon
  • Breastplate of Armageddon
  • The feather from a Harpy
  • The skin of an Elephant
  • A large pearl from an UnderDark clam
  • 250000 in drakaan

Player walkthroughs

Five separate quests from older player notes. Step-by-step text stays in spoilers. Rules: Quest system.

  • Threshold — Nosem
  • Nymph Forest — leprechaun
  • Merevia grasslands — hunter
  • Roshander — dragonoid king
  • Psionics trainer

Threshold — Nosem

Spoiler: Nosem in Threshold 
***'provide assistance'
Nosem says in common : Thank you for offering help!  I am currently looking
                       for a friend of mine.  She was supposed to meet me
                       here in Threshold, but I have not been able to find 
                       her.  She was coming to Threshold in a ship that
                       left from Kalinport.  If you see her, please 'say' to
                       her the following message :  

                       'Nosem is in the local tavern'

                       She should then be able to find me, hopefully.
                       Thanks for the help, again!

   (NOTE: You must type 'say'.  Do not use an alias or the ' for speaking)

***Find 'Traveller' in Threshold docks and 'say Nosem is in the local tavern'

Nymph Forest — leprechaun

Spoiler: Leprechaun in Nymph Forest 
***Find the Leprechaun in nymph forest. he's in the se corner, and doesnt move. but does hide.

The leprechaun says in common : You got me, but I don't have any gold.  An old
                    dryad named Amalthea stole it from me.  She lives in her
                    tree in the oak forest, west of the lake.

***kill amalthea
Before dying the old dryad whispers, 'If you want the leprechaun's gold you
can find it on top of the cliff southeast of here, inside a hole'.

***Find the room 'Nymph Forest : On a cliff (n, w).' and 'search hole'

Merevia grasslands — hunter

Spoiler: Merevia grasslands hunter 
***find hunter in merevia grasslands and type 'ask about help'
The hunter tells you : I hear there is a fearsome troll that lives below 
ground in these grasslands.  I'm told that he has a really fine two-handed 
club.  If you could bring me that club you would be greatly rewarded for your 
efforts.  If you have any questions, just ask me.

***go to Grassland entrance (n, e, w, ne, nw). then go 'n;nw' and 'pass through tracks'
(it will be dark in here, so you might need a torch) find the troll in this place,
kill him, take his club, take it back and give it to the hunter

Roshander — dragonoid king

Spoiler: Roshander and the dragonoid king 
go to roshander, when finding the dragonoid kings lair
find "A path encircling an elevated platform (e, nw)."
then 'search bush' then 'push button' then head n,nw until you find a 'opening'
enter the opening, then leave and find roshandar. 'assist roshandar' again

Psionics trainer
