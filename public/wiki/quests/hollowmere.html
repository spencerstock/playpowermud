Horn of the Elder Heroes (Castle Hollowmere)

From help quests_list (2026-11). Siege/defence quest on the Plains of Nereka. Marked very hard; a group is best. Rules: Quest system.

Summary

Castle Hollowmere, on the road east of the Plains of Nereka, is under siege by the Hollow Host. Steward Maren Holt starts the siege clock. Gather the three pieces of the founders' oath, open the Cenotaph in the crypt, and blow the Horn of the Elder Heroes on the battlefield before the Lord Commander falls.

Follow-up (only after Hollowmere has fallen to the Host): The Commander's Daughter — Lord Gerrick Thane, Castle Ravenhold, do not march the daughter out through the gates.

Getting there

  • From the Plains of Nereka, take the unlisted road east ( There is one road leading east to Castle Hollowmere.) into the rutted Hollow Road and gatehouse chain.
  • Inner castle: through the outer bailey and inner gate to the great hall. Steward's office is north of the hall; the library is west; Battle Gate and the field lie to the south of the inner square.
  • Bellwarden Tomas (bell tower) tracks time: ask when the battle begins. A countdown dial in the tower marks the coming of the Host.

Recorded dialogue

Transcript from live play (2026-11). Full text is spoilered — same policy as Quest notes.

Steward Maren Holt

Spoiler: ask Maren about the siege, Host, Skarra Vael, and the horn 
>>> ask maren about the siege
Maren says: The Hollow Host marches on us, orcs, goblins, kobolds, hobgoblins and worse, led by a
war-witch called Skarra Vael.  We have thirty-odd soldiers.  They have thousands.  They will not
stop and they will not tire.

>>> ask maren about the host
Maren says: The Hollow Host.  They come from the black hills to the south.  They are replaced faster
than we can kill them.  We cannot win a fair fight.

>>> ask maren about skarra vael
Maren says: Skarra Vael is a war-witch, a human gone bad.  She keeps the Host's banner of the Hollow Eye
and a loathsome pavilion behind the hills.  They say she keeps a stolen banner of ours there.

>>> ask steward about horn
Maren says: The Horn of the Elder Heroes.  Our founders swore an oath that if Hollowmere were ever in
dire need, the Heroes from the Past would ride again to defend it.  The horn is the key.  Nobody knows
where it is.  I think it lies in the crypt below the chapel, behind a seal that three pieces of the
founders' oath will open.

Library Wren

Spoiler: ask Wren about the founders' oath and the three pieces 
>>> ask wren about the founders' oath
Wren says: The founders' oath.  Three founders swore it: the Vanguard, the Warden and the Herald.  Each
gave a piece: a stone, a seal and a banner.  They are scattered.

Wren pushes her spectacles up her nose.  "That is in none of my books, dear.  I can tell you about the
founders' oath, the three founders, the Vanguard's stone, the last line of the oath, Ser Calder, the
Warden's seal, the Herald's banner, or the Steward's letter.  Try 'ask wren about oath' to begin."

>>> ask wren about the three founders
Wren says: Aldous the First, Brenna Ironhand and Ser Calder the Fallen.  They built the castle and swore
to protect it forever.

>>> ask wren about the vanguard's stone
Wren says: The Vanguard's Oath-Stone is hidden in the Garden of Heroes, under the statue of the first
to fall.  You must say the last line of the oath at the statue, with 'recite we stand where they stood'
(or 'chant' or 'intone') to open the hiding place.  The last line is: 'We stand where they stood.'

>>> ask wren about the last line of the oath
Wren says: The founders' oath.  Three founders swore it: the Vanguard, the Warden and the Herald.  Each
gave a piece: a stone, a seal and a banner.  They are scattered.

>>> ask wren about Ser Calder
Wren says: Ser Calder the Fallen.  He fell first.  His statue stands in the Garden of Heroes.  It is the
second on the left.

>>> ask wren about the Warden's seal
Wren says: The Warden's Seal is in the west flank, in the watchtower the Warden built.  The Warden's ghost
still guards it.

>>> ask wren about the herald's banner
Wren says: The Herald's Banner was stolen.  I believe Skarra Vael has it.  It will be cursed, and the
chapel's sisters must cleanse it.

>>> ask wren about the steward's letter
Wren says: The Steward's letter will tell you where to begin.  Read it carefully.

Object checklist (from dialogue)

  • Vanguard's Oath-Stone — Garden of Heroes; recite the last line at the right statue (Ser Calder: second on the left).
  • Warden's Seal — west-flank watchtower; Warden's ghost.
  • Herald's Banner — stolen; likely with Skarra Vael; cleanse at chapel.
  • Horn — crypt below chapel; seal opened with the three oath pieces.
  • Steward's letter — starting pointer (read carefully; not transcribed here yet).
