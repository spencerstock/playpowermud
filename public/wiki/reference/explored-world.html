Explored map data

Play Powermud ships a starting map of Powermud and grows it as you walk around. Your personal discoveries stay on this browser; the shared file updates when the site is redeployed.

Shared baseline

  • /data/powermud.graph.json — rooms and links many players have already mapped
  • New deploys may ship a newer graph; your browser merges it if the file’s updatedAt is newer

Your map

  • Every room you visit can add nodes and exits to IndexedDB on this device
  • See World map for how to open the panel and read the legend

Static route guides

Long text and HTML direction dumps (whole zones spelled out exit-by-exit) live in the maintainer corpus. For day-to-day play, prefer the live map plus world overview notes.
