Learn your way around Powermud: directions

Spencer's 2008 walking list. Routes are grouped by starting city, in the original order. Each step block is copied as written, including the original typos in the steps.

The place in parentheses at the start of a block is where the route begins. Other parentheses are landmarks from the list. Phrases in asterisks, such as *pry floorboard*, are commands rather than compass moves. Safety comments from the list are repeated under each route that had one.

These are old player notes. Confirm exits in game, or on your map, before you trust a path. The list itself says some Merevia areas are missing, and that Samurai Village is inaccessible.
