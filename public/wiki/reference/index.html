  • Items & equipment — catalogs, drops, sac/plus, verified sources

  • World & directions — Threshold, zones, HTML/txt route dumps

Also in corpus: code-and-functions/ (LPC name dump), client/ (Ashavar AL Client archives), misc/, archives/ (original zips).
