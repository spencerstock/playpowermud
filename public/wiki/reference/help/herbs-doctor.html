Doctor, herbs & intensify

From in-game help (doctor, succor, harvest, intensify). Verify plants live before harvesting.

Doctor profession

  • Requires 12 INT, 12 DEX; lower path: Herbalist.
  • Strong succor and natural regeneration; raises caps for First aid, Intensification, Harvesting.
  • Shorter wait between succor; can use stronger medical components.

Harvesting

  • harvest with no target — in rooms with gatherable plants.
  • Cooldown: “You must wait a moment” between tries; area depletion: “harvested too much recently.”
  • On success, get all if loot is not automatic.

Succor (First aid)

succor · succor <target> · succor <target> with <component>

Removes bleeding on success; components (bandages, herbs, crafted tiers) improve odds.

intensify <type> on <target> or on me — Doctor and/or Herbalist only for each type.
