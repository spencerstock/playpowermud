Celtian Battlemages

From in-game help battlemage and help guilds (2026-10-30).

Guild summary

LocationCeltia (north of Dunagin)
Races allowedHuman, Half-Elf, Half-Orc, Minotaur
Ability minimums15 INT, 10 WIS, 13 CON, 11 STR, 11 DEX
SchoolsAlteration, Conjuration, Divination, Evocation
Guild skillsUnarmed, Perception, Swim, Climb, Blunt, Pierce, Slash, Dodging, Ranged, slash/pierce/blunt aversion

Battlemages are fighter-wizards: strong spellcasters who still train for melee. Most spare time goes to warfare training.

Joining any guild

From help guilds:

  • You need at least 500 experience before joining a guild (merchants excepted).
  • Not all skills transfer — e.g. slash learned before joining a mage guild may no longer apply. Many players focus blunt or pierce in the Newbie Arena while planning a guild.

Joining (player notes — not in official help)

After restat, 500+ exp, and ability mins: at Dunagin docks kill the Receiving Clerk for his quill pen; bring it to the Celtian Battlemages guild in Celtia (north of Dunagin).
