Player races & abilities

From in-game help races and selected race help (pulled 2026-10-30).

All playable races

help races lists:

drow, duergar, dwarf, elf, half-elf, halfling, half-orc, half-ogre, human, gnome, minotaur, svirfneblin

Battlemage-eligible: Human, Half-Elf, Half-Orc, Minotaur — see Celtian Battlemages for guild requirements.

Exact per-race stat rolls are shown by restat in the Adventurer Guild, not in help files.

Human

  • Advantages / disadvantages: none (baseline race).
  • Short-lived compared to other races; generalist adventurers.

Half-elf

  • Above average Wisdom; below average Charisma.
  • Resistances: Enchant (high), Illusion (low).

Half-orc

  • Above average Strength and Constitution; below average Intelligence and Charisma.
  • Resistance: Dragonfear (low).

Minotaur

  • Very high Strength, above average Constitution; very low Wisdom and Charisma.
  • Resistance: Dragonfear (high). Horned — special helmet types only.
  • Marked for experienced Powermud players in help.
