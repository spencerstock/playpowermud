Eset & ANSI (in-game)

From help ansi, help eset, help variables. Browser rendering: Terminal & colors.

  • eset ansi on — required for channel colors and combat ANSI
  • eset <var> <flags> <color> — e.g. eset tell bold blue
  • eunset <var>

Colors: black, red, green, yellow, blue, purple, cyan, white. Common vars: tell, say, shout, announce, guild, groupchat, fields, emote.
