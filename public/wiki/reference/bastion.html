Bastion of High Sorcery

The Bastion is Powermud's guild of general wizards, with seven schools of magic including Enchantment and Illusion, plus scribing. Getting in is a small quest of its own: first you have to find the guild, then bring its doorkeeper a gift. This page tells the story of how we did it. Hints start vague and get stronger, and anything that gives the answer away is hidden. Click a Spoiler bar to reveal it.

Who can join

RacesMinimum statsWhere
Human, Elf, Half-ElfInt 15, Wis 13A moving tower in the forests west of Threshold (a second mirror is rumoured south of Elinost)
  • Like most guilds, you need about 500 exp before you can join. Our character was an elf from the Adventurer's Guild with just over 500 exp.
  • If you want to restat, do it before you join a guild. It only works in the Adventurer's Guild. See Stats, restat and hometowns.
  • A mage guild can lose fighting skills such as Slash. Read help bastion in game before you commit. Joining is hard to undo.

Finding the Bastion

The Bastion doesn't have a front door in any town. Its way in is through a mirror.

  • Hint 1: Leave Threshold to the west, past the guard at the forest's edge, and follow the forest trails away from the Caravan Route. Pay attention when a room description mentions something strange off in the distance.
  • Hint 2: You're looking for a black tower that has no business being in the middle of the woods. Squirrels will scold you as you get close.
Spoiler: where the tower is

From the Caravan Route intersection just west of Threshold, take the small forest trail west until it splits three ways, then turn south. Follow the path south and southwest until the forest says it can see "some sort of structure off to the west, looking like a tower." Go west. You land in front of an "ominous black tower". Type tower to go in, then u to reach the top floor.

Spoiler: getting through the mirror

The Obsidian Mirror is on the tower's clean top floor. The command is exactly jump through the mirror. Shorter versions such as jump mirror and enter mirror don't work. On the other side you land "on a hard stone floor" inside the Bastion.

The price of entry

Inside the Bastion, look around the meeting hall. The mage who decides who gets in won't do it for free.

Spoiler: who to talk to, and what he wants

Jerem stands in the western meeting hall (go w from where you land). He says:

Jerem says : I sure could use a Golden Amulet! If you give me one, and your abilities are high enough, then I will let you in the Bastion. It is said that the amulet is located underneath the Human settlement of Threshold, in the well itself, not the catacombs.

"Abilities" most likely means the minimum stats above (Int 15, Wis 13). We haven't confirmed that.

Finding the Golden Amulet

  • Hint 1: The doorkeeper's hint is exact. The amulet is under Threshold, in a well, and not in the catacombs. Most of the town gets its water from newer wells.
  • Hint 2: Look in Threshold's roughest, darkest alley. One stretch of it is "somewhat more lit" than the rest.
  • Hint 3: Going down is harder than pressing d. Try spelling out what you want to do with the well.
  • Hint 4: At the bottom, don't go looking for passages. Look closely at the well itself. Its walls are packed stone and mortar.
Spoiler: where the well is

The well is in Kraken Alley. Walk Frontiers Street toward the town square. Just west of the square, by a small weather-beaten house, Kraken Alley runs south. Go s twice to reach the lit stretch with the town well.

Spoiler: getting down the well

Type down the well. Plain d doesn't work. You climb down the handholds into ankle-deep brackish water.

Spoiler: getting the amulet (full answer) 
l at stone
pry stone
get amulet

That gives you the Golden Amulet. Climb back out with up.

A trap in the well

Low-level characters: be careful at the bottom of the well. There's something there you can trigger by accident, and it drops you somewhere you really don't want to be. Read the next spoiler if you'd rather not find out the hard way.
Spoiler: the metal plate

The small metal plate under the water is a red herring. step on plate rotates a wall and dumps you into the catacombs. The amulet isn't there, and the catacombs hold a Hydra (east of the rat hole) and Alligators that attack on sight. They're far too strong for a new character. Ours dropped to 27 HP before getting out. If you end up there by mistake, step on the plate on the southern wall to get back to the well.

From the well to the Bastion

With the amulet in hand, head back to the tower.

Spoiler: full walking route, well bottom to the mirror 
up
n; n
w; w; w; w; w        (along Frontiers Street, out of town)
sw; nw               (to the Caravan Route intersection)
w; w; w; w           (forest trail to the three-way split)
s; s; sw; w          (south to the tower)
tower
u                    (top floor, the Obsidian Mirror)
jump through the mirror

Our walk was quiet, with no fights, but other wanderers may be about.

Joining

Take the amulet back to the mage who asked for it. He should let you in, as long as your abilities are high enough.
