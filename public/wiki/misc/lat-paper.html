review paper

The page reads:

After years of research, I believe I have uncovered the truth about the demise
of the great Hotius Lat.  The report of the great Mage dying in a battle with
the great Silver Dragon of Celtia is at last proven to be false.

It is true, however, that Lat was indeed working with a group of veteran
dragon killers on dispatching the aforementioned beast.  His death came before
any battle, though.

In my research I have found that Lat was hired by a group of 5 individuals of
various talents to assist them in the killing of the Dragon.  A Dwarf named
Balgar was the elected leader of the group.  The group met in Deepwater at
the famous Cliff House.  One member of the group, an elf with a mysterious
background but impeccably and royally dressed, ordered the Dragon Steak as
a joke.  Evidently this was alluding to their mission.  Lat met the group
there and it was planned to leave at first light.  In the middle of the
night, the entire party was tossed from the Inn.  This was because another
member of the group, a female half-ogre of frightening size, almost tore
up the place in a fit of drunken rage.

Luckily, a member of the party owned a ship in Deepwater, so they were able
to cast off that night.  This sailor, a female Minotaur, easily sailed the
ship to Dunagin, a port on Celtia.  During this journey, the 5 other members
of the party could not help but notice the beauty and obvious value of the
rings that Lat wore on his fingers.  These rings, of course, were the
legendary Rings of Lat, his own creations.  Each ring had the wonderous
ability to shield the wearer from 7 different types of Melee attacks.  The
eigth ring, the Purple one, was the most valuable of all.  That ring alone
would protect against the five types of melee attacks, while the other
rings only proteced against one form of melee damage each.

After a night's stay at the Cap'n Hook's pub, the party headed off towards
the lair of the great Beast.  They travelled out of the northeastern gates and
headed in that general direction.  One member of the group, a dangerous
Drow Mercenary, lead the way.  He had evidently been close to the lair in
previous trips to Celtia.  It is well-known that this Drow was a Renegade,
and did not reside in Nal Shaddar.

The party reached the base of the high cliff where the Silver Dragon had
its lair.  They decided to wait until first light to attack, and try to
catch the Dragon while it was sleeping.

During the night, an odd being rushed directly into camp and attacked
Lat with a ferocity that caught the Enchanter off guard.  The being was
using his claws and jaw to attack Lat, evidently getting around all of his
rings' protections.  The magician was dragged away into the bush by this
being, with the other 5 members of the party in hot pursuit.

Lat's body was found about one hundred feet away, and he was quite dead.
The other members of the party, scared out of their wits, decided to not
continue with the killing of the Dragon.

Being oppurtunists, though, they set about getting from Lat's corpse whatever
they could.  After all, he was dead and did not need anything!

They found nothing on the dead man save five of the aforementioned rings.
The Purple and most valuable of the rings had been taken by that being.
Two of the other rings were also missing.
They decided to each take a ring, and to go their separate ways.

Lat was obviously a great and powerful Enchanter, and now the entire story
of his life is now complete.


     - Varien Rogthat, Historian

Rings of Lat — chart

The paper names eight rings (seven single-type aversions plus Purple). Known singleton colors and aversion lines from old Magisk eq / player checks (not full judge text for every color). None of these rings use resistance to claws the way Animalic Leggings do — claws and jaw on Lat were the attack that bypassed his protections in the story. Closest documented ring for beasts is Silver (biting aversion).

Hunters vs drops

The five who split corpse loot: Balgar (dwarf leader), royal elf, half-ogre, minotaur sailor, renegade drow mercenary. Only Balgar → Yellow is hub-confirmed. Pairing the other corpse rings to {green/cyan/red/blue} colors is guesswork until corpses are seen again. Silver and the two “missing” rings from the paper are plausibly not among those five.

Legacy NPC ↔ item lines from 2008 are on Legacy magical drops (2008) and merged into Magical drops where marked legacy 2008.
