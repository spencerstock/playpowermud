World map

Live mapper while you play — builds a world graph from room text, entirely in your browser.

Open the map

  • Alt+M (Option+M on macOS) expands or collapses the panel.
  • On mobile, tap Map.

What you see

  • Your position updates as room text arrives from the MUD.
  • Levels — use ▲/▼ in the map toolbar to change z (up/down in the world).
  • Follow — when on, the view stays centered on you; panning or selecting another room turns it off.
  • Search — filter mapped rooms by title, description, tags, or NPC names.
  • Room details — click a room for exits, contents, and links.
  • Legend — dashed = inferred link; markers for up/down and unexplored exits.

Click-drag empty map space to pan. Focus the map canvas and use arrow keys to move the selection cursor between rooms (navigation only — does not send movement commands).

This client does not click-to-walk, speedwalk, or auto-path.

Shipped map vs your discoveries

The site ships a Powermud world graph at /data/powermud.graph.json (a community baseline). As you explore, new rooms and links merge into your local copy. If a newer shipped file arrives (newer updatedAt), new baseline rooms are merged in without wiping what you mapped.

Your learned map is saved in IndexedDB (playpowermud database) on this browser.

How mapping works (short)

Each command you send is queued; each room display is matched to the command that caused it. The mapper:

  • Identifies rooms by title, exits, and description (brief and verbose look map to the same room).
  • Learns compass, vertical, named, and secret exits as you traverse them.
  • Re-localizes when dead reckoning disagrees with room text, so the pin stays on the right room.
  • Periodically heals duplicate rooms and bad links (on load and as you move).

Updating the shipped graph

Site deploys include public/data/powermud.graph.json. When maintainers publish a newer graph, your browser merges it if the file’s updatedAt is newer than your saved base.
