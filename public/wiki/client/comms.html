Comms panel

Tells, room says, and shouts in a side panel — guild chat and announce lines stay in the main log.

Open the panel

  • Alt+C (Option+C on macOS) toggles expand/collapse.
  • On mobile, tap Comms in the bottom bar.

What gets captured

  • Tells — incoming and your outgoing tells
  • Says — room speech (yours always; others only if they are in the client’s known-player list)
  • Shouts — always shown

Each line has a timestamp. The panel keeps up to 400 recent entries.

Powermud channels ([guild], group chat, fields, etc.) are not copied into this panel — read them in the main terminal (use eset colors from Terminal & colors).

Per character

When the logged-in character changes, the comms list switches to that character’s saved history in localStorage on this browser. Nothing syncs across devices.

If a tell or say is missing here, check the main log — parsing waits for a full line, and some lines are filtered (e.g. replay blocks, announce spam).
