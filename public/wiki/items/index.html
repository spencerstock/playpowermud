Items

Plus is an item's magical power, not a weapon skill. The lists on this wiki are player catalogs of gear people had seen in game. Normal and magic equipment pages also show merged hub columns when stats are vendored: judge verdict, sell price, who drops an item, and chance drop rates.

  • Magic items — slot, plus, effects, plus hub judge/sell/drop columns
  • Normal equipment — mundane gear, quality, where seen, plus hub judge/sell/drop columns
  • Crafting components
  • Shop stock — vendor list snapshots
  • Magical drops — which NPCs were seen dropping which items
