About this wiki

Player notes and copied in-game help. Powermud itself is the authority when a page and the game disagree.

Spoilers

Quest solutions and similar giveaways sit in closed spoiler bars. Wiki search leaves those bodies out.

Left out

  • Personal character sheets, exp logs, and gear lists tied to one character
  • Proxy addresses and other multi-connection notes
  • Client config, triggers, and aliases from any one MUD client
  • Cheats and exploits

The game asks players not to share quest solutions. The quest notes stay closed for that reason.

Open source

The Play Powermud browser client, relay, and this wiki are open source on GitHub under the MIT license. Pull requests and issue reports are welcome.
