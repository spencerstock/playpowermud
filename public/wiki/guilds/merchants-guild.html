Merchant's Guild

Trade guild in Powermud. Join at 500+ exp (Merchants are the exception). At the recruiter, type info for the joining task. In-game: help merchant.

RacesMinimum statsWhere
AllCha 11, Int 10Kalinport, Deepwater, Kal-Thax, Nal Shaddar, Dunkrist, Granei

Haggling, appraising, brewing potions, alchemy and concoctions; sells at much better rates. The only guild you can join before 500 exp.

In-game help marks this guild as suited to experienced players.
