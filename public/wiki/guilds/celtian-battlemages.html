Celtian Battlemages

Hybrids guild in Powermud. Join at 500+ exp (Merchants are the exception). At the recruiter, type info for the joining task. In-game: help battlemage.

RacesMinimum statsWhere
Human, Half-Elf, Half-Orc, MinotaurInt 15, Con 13, Str 11, Dex 11, Wis 10Gloriath Castle, north of Dunagin

Fighter-wizards with slash, blunt, pierce and ranged plus Alteration, Conjuration, Divination and Evocation. Join by bringing the quill pen from the Receiving Clerk at the Dunagin docks. Spells include Burning Hands (10 SP), Shocking Grasp (15), Fireball (45) and Blade Circle (60).
