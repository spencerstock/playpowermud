Play

From in-game guild help. Join at 500+ exp (Merchants are the exception). At the recruiter, type info for the joining task. Each guild below has the same fields: races, minimum stats, where, and a short description.

  • Threshold Militia — Town defenders with charging, dual wield and offhand. Members pay less for most Threshold services.
  • Knights of Nereka — Mounted knights with riding, lancing, shield bash, triple attack and advanced defense. Three orders (Circle, Blade, Kingfisher); the top order gets clerical spells from Kertaine. The knighthood distrusts non-clerical magic.
  • Elinost Elite — Guards of the Speaker of the Moon. Advanced melee, dual and offhand, plus hide and sneak.
  • Stronghold Honor Guard — Dwarven defenders with dual, triple, offhand and advanced melee.
  • Dunkrist Homeguard — Ambush fighters: backstab, dual, unarmed dual and triple, and epic slash, blunt and unarmed.
  • Melee-Magthere — Drow warrior school: dual, advanced melee and unarmed, hide, sneak and backstab.
  • Northmen — Barbarians of Bhalt with natural defense, unarmed up to quadruple and epic slash.
  • Mariner — Sailors and brawlers: sailing, fishing, unarmed up to quadruple, epic pierce. Bonus to sailing and resistance to water damage.
  • Monks of Aber — Unarmed masters (up to quadruple), evasion, hide, sneak and natural defense. Very limited armour. Strongest when neutral; they regenerate without food or drink.
  • Highlander Clan — Immortal swordsmen. Help says the clan is closed to new members.

Trade

GuildRacesMinimum statsWhere
Merchant's GuildAllCha 11, Int 10Kalinport, Deepwater, Kal-Thax, Nal Shaddar, Dunkrist, Granei
  • Merchant's Guild — Haggling, appraising, brewing potions, alchemy and concoctions; sells at much better rates. The only guild you can join before 500 exp.
Skills don't always carry over. Some skills stop working when you join a guild. For example, a mage guild loses Slash, so check help <guild> before you train a weapon type in the arena. Blunt and Pierce carry into almost every guild.
