Rangers

Hybrids guild in Powermud. Join at 500+ exp (Merchants are the exception). At the recruiter, type info for the joining task. In-game: help ranger.

RacesMinimum statsWhere
Human, Half-Elf, Elf, HalflingDex 15, Wis 14, Con 13, Str 13Great Forest (west of Threshold, east of Elinost), southern Merevia

Followers of Felli: ranged and two-weapon fighting without penalty, plus skinning, fishing, cooking, fire starting, riding and woodworking. No guild house; recruiters are Rexiate (woods west of Threshold), Lorn (forests east of Elinost) and Greeu (southern shore of Merevia).

In-game help marks this guild as suited to experienced players.
