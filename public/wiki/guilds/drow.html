Noble houses and Bey-Sandrax

Drow belong to noble houses in Nal Shaddar. One player got into House Bey-Sandrax as a non-noble member by asking an admin by tell. The admin then said to request a medallion in the house join room.

  1. Enter through the gates. From the gates, go northeast to the room 'almost directly below a large marble balcony'.
  2. Climb to the balcony. l at balcony says you can climb it, or reach it while levitating. climb up works ('After a bit of scrambling…'), and climb down brings you back.
  3. Go in. in leads to the Nobles' sanctuary. It has a Small plaque, the Donation Scale, a Bulletin Board and three LOCKED adamantite doors. North is a hallway with three more locked doors.
  4. Request your medallion. request medallion in the sanctuary gives a platinum medallion. It answered 'What ?' in the courtyard and temple rooms.
  5. Wear it and use its powers. wear medallion, then medallion list: protect (protection from good), bless, darkness and levitate. Use medallion <power> in lower case.
  • list at the plaque — Shows the house roster: the Matron Mother, priestesses, House Mage, Weaponmaster, Battle Generals and the non-nobles.
  • Donation Scale — Takes drakaan donations to the house. peruse its sign for details.
  • Bulletin Board — 25 old posts. A 2008 post says a member paid for a house smithy so 'repairing is much cheaper'.
  • Rest of the compound — Temple of Loth to the east (an altar 'covered in what looks like blood'), commoners' barracks to the north, and a smithy exit by the gates.
Think before you commit. House commands like joining and forsake house can't be undone.

House smith — repair and resize

A well-trained Drow warrior smith serves House Bey-Sandrax in the house smithy (exit by the gates). l at smith notes that he repairs armor and can resize it. Ask with ask smith about repairing or ask smith about resizing (lower case).

Resizing only works on armor that has not been resized before — a second resize weakens the material. Quote a job with rprice <armor> to <size>, then resize <armor> to <size> to have it done.
