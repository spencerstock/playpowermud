Church of Kertaine

Clerics guild in Powermud. Join at 500+ exp (Merchants are the exception). At the recruiter, type info for the joining task. In-game: help kertaine.

RacesMinimum statsWhere
Human, Half-Elf, Half-Orc, Halfling, DwarfWis 14, Int 11Main church in Deepwater; also Threshold (Kalinport's doesn't take joiners)

Clerics of Kertaine: faith, blunt, ranged; Necromancy, Abjuration, Conjuration, Divination.
