Monks of Aber

Fighters guild in Powermud. Join at 500+ exp (Merchants are the exception). At the recruiter, type info for the joining task. In-game: help monks.

RacesMinimum statsWhere
HumanStr 15, Wis 15, Dex 15, Con 11Absinthe Mountains, between Threshold and Kal-Thax

Unarmed masters (up to quadruple), evasion, hide, sneak and natural defense. Very limited armour. Strongest when neutral; they regenerate without food or drink.
