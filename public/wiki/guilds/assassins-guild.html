Assassin's Guild

Stealth guild in Powermud. Join at 500+ exp (Merchants are the exception). At the recruiter, type info for the joining task. In-game: help assassin.

RacesMinimum statsWhere
Human, Half-Elf, Half-Orc, Half-Ogre, Halfling, Drow, Duergar, Gnome, Minotaur, SvirfneblinDex 17, Str 15Kalinport (under the Dune Ave manhole); branch in Dunkrist

The School of Stealth: hide, sneak, assassination, dodging, offhand. Dinien recruits and trains in Kalinport. Hide costs 750 and assassinate 2,000 drakaan to learn.

In-game help marks this guild as suited to experienced players.
