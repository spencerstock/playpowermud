Dragon notes

Short location notes from a player guide. Confirm the rooms in game.

  • Brass — entangles.
  • White — north part of Cutland's Pass: find the snow leopard, then climb up. Uses fire, ice, and acid.
  • Lead — by the Viluten mountains, near Geoffrey. One recorded approach is e, enter gorge, enter fissure, e, e, se.
  • Xotan — plasma globe, nova flare (blinds), energy weakness, and stuns.

Merevia

Names that show up together in Merevia hunting notes: Brenoak, vampire, neksarb, parabus, lord lorasin, ghost of gatherus, barbakas.
