Languages

NPC language trainers sell new tongues for drakaan. At a trainer, list shows prices and learn <language> buys one you do not already know. Type languages to see what you speak. When someone uses a language you do not know, the game often shows “says something in an unknown language” instead of the words ( Comms follows the same rule for other players).

Knowing a language matters for quests and NPC keywords — for example some lines require human ( quest notes). Speech uses the in-language form, e.g. say in common : hello.

Demetrii, Threshold

Demetrii the sage in the House of Demetrii on northern Gateway Road, Threshold (beginner’s guide). read sign and list at the House. Menu from in-game list:

Language Cost to learn
Dwarf 8,000
Halfling 2,500
Human 1,000
Orc 3,000

Wise gnome, Gnomish Citadel

Wise gnome is below the Citadel town: on the east side of town, enter trapdoor (same entrance as the Frokk / psionics chain in quest notes). Then list and learn <language> as usual:

Language Cost to learn
Gnome 8,000
Human 1,500
Orc 5,000
Pixie 15,000

Illusionists join in the Citadel at Granei ( Illusionists guild). Bobe sells potions in the Citadel too ( beginner’s guide).

Alleena, Elinost

Alleena in Elinost — the language trainer Frokk and quest dialogue call a young girl in Elinost (quest notes). From north of the first Elite guards (Elinost Elite area): climb up, n, climb down, open door, s. Her list menu is not recorded on this wiki yet.
