Professions

Specialisations you can learn after a guild. They add or boost skills, sometimes at a cost to others. Lower professions are easier. Each upper profession needs a related lower one first. Every profession has its own help topic.

TierProfessions
LowerAlchemist, Animal Trainer, Aristocrat, Artificer, Auger, Bard, Brawler, Chef, Dragon Hunter, Herbalist, Inditar, Lumberjack, Miner, Muse, Outdoorsman, Sailor, Salesman, Scholar, Tactician, Tumbler, Undead Hunter
UpperAcrobat, Archer, Blacksmith, Bladesman, Blind-Fighter, Carpenter, Doctor, Dragon Slayer, Duelist, Gladiator, Horseman, Politician, Sapper, Shikari, Spy, Troubadour, Undead Slayer
Psionic onlyAegis, Afrontist, Lucidor, Mentalist, Phrenic

Examples of upper paths: Herbalist then Doctor (needs Int 12 and Dex 12; adds succor, harvest and intensify), Tumbler then Acrobat, Dragon Hunter then Dragon Slayer, Artificer or Miner then Blacksmith, Bard then Troubadour.
