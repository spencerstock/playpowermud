Profession trainers

Professions are taught by NPC trainers in cities and guild areas. At a trainer, use list to see costs and stat requirements, then learn <profession> when you have enough gold and the right abilities. Each profession also has help <profession> in game. Tier lists: Professions.

This page is for profession trainers only (Animal trainer, Herbalist, Brawler, and similar). For riding, tailoring, forging, and other standalone skills, see Skill trainers.

This page records trainer locations as players map them. Costs are in drakaan unless a trainer says otherwise.

Kelton (Merevia)

The professions trainer in Kelton on Merevia teaches the following (from in-game list):

Profession Cost to learn Abilities required
Animal trainer 10,000 Dexterity 12 and Intelligence 11
Horseman 30,000 Dexterity 14
Outdoorsman 20,000 Dexterity 10

Tiers: Animal trainer and Outdoorsman are lower professions; Horseman is an upper profession and typically needs a related lower profession first (see help horseman and help professions in game).

Dunagin

The professions trainer in Dunagin teaches the following (from in-game list):

Profession Cost to learn Abilities required
Doctor 25,000 Intelligence 12 and Dexterity 12
Herbalist 20,000 Intelligence 10 and Wisdom 10
Muse 30,000 Wisdom 11

Tiers: Herbalist is the usual lower path before Doctor (see help doctor). Wiki detail on herbs and healing: Doctor & herbs.

Deepwater

Deepwater has one Professions Trainer. From in-game list or list professions:

Profession Cost to learn Abilities required
Archer 60,000 Dexterity 15
Bladesman 60,000 Dexterity 14
Blind-fighter 40,000 Dexterity 14
Brawler 15,000 Strength 14 and Dexterity 12
Duelist 60,000 Dexterity 12 and Strength 12
Sapper 55,000 Strength 13 and Dexterity 11
Tactician 25,000 Intelligence 11 and Strength 11

Tiers: Brawler and Tactician are lower professions; Archer, Bladesman, Blind-fighter, Duelist, and Sapper are upper — each needs the matching lower path first (see individual help topics). The Jockey in Deepwater teaches Riding as a skill, not a profession.

Kalinport

Professions Annex (exits west and east). One Professions Trainer. From in-game list or list professions:

Profession Cost to learn Abilities required
Acrobat 70,000 Dexterity 15 and Constitution 13
Alchemist 25,000 Dexterity 12
Aristocrat 20,000 Intelligence 11 and Charisma 10
Bard 20,000 Charisma 10 and Dexterity 12
Gladiator 60,000 Dexterity 14 and Charisma 12
Politician 40,000 Charisma 12 and Intelligence 12
Sailor 20,000 Dexterity 12 and Strength 12
Salesman 30,000 Intelligence 12
Troubadour 40,000 Charisma 12 and Dexterity 13
Tumbler 10,000 Dexterity 13

Tiers: Tumbler and Bard are lower paths; Acrobat and Troubadour are upper (see individual help topics). Alchemist, Aristocrat, Gladiator, Politician, Sailor, and Salesman are standalone or mixed-tier — check help professions in game. The Riding Instructor in Kalinport teaches Riding as a skill, not a profession.

Crossroads

Professions Annex (exits west and southeast). One Professions Trainer. From in-game list or list professions:

Profession Cost to learn Abilities required
Artificer 10,000 Dexterity 12
Blacksmith 25,000 Strength 14
Carpenter 15,000 Dexterity 13
Chef 10,000 Dexterity 10 and Intelligence 10
Lumberjack 10,000 Strength 12
Miner 10,000 Strength 14

Tiers: Artificer, Chef, Lumberjack, and Miner are lower professions; Blacksmith and Carpenter are upper — Artificer or Miner before Blacksmith (see help blacksmith and help carpenter).

Toril

Professions Annex (exits east and west). One Professions Trainer. From in-game list or list professions:

Profession Cost to learn Abilities required
Shikari 60,000 Wisdom 12 and Intelligence 12

Underdark — psionic professions

The Psionic Professions Trainer teaches the psionic-only profession tier (see Professions — tiers). Mapped room trail: An ending in the tunnel (south, east; Barregalla) → A small alcove (east, west; Psionics' Bulletin Board) → A small room (west). Entry is usually via the psionics quest chain. From in-game list or list professions:

Profession Cost to learn Abilities required
Aegis 100,000 None
Afrontist 100,000 None
Lucidor 100,000 None
Mentalist 100,000 None
Phrenic 150,000 None

Tiers: These five are the psionic only row on the master list; they do not appear at city Professions Annex trainers. Check help professions for how they interact with guild and combat limits.
