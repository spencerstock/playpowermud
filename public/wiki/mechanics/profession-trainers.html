Profession trainers

Professions are taught by NPC trainers in cities and guild areas. At a trainer, use list to see costs and stat requirements, then learn <profession> when you have enough gold and the right abilities. Each profession also has help <profession> in game. Overview of tiers: Beginner's guide — Professions.

This page records trainer locations as players map them. Costs are in drakaan unless a trainer says otherwise.

Kelton (Merevia)

The professions trainer in Kelton on Merevia teaches the following (from in-game list):

Profession Cost to learn Abilities required
Animal trainer 10,000 Dexterity 12 and Intelligence 11
Horseman 30,000 Dexterity 14
Outdoorsman 20,000 Dexterity 10

Tiers: Animal trainer and Outdoorsman are lower professions; Horseman is an upper profession and typically needs a related lower profession first (see help horseman and help professions in game).

Dunagin

The professions trainer in Dunagin teaches the following (from in-game list):

Profession Cost to learn Abilities required
Doctor 25,000 Intelligence 12 and Dexterity 12
Herbalist 20,000 Intelligence 10 and Wisdom 10
Muse 30,000 Wisdom 11

Tiers: Herbalist is the usual lower path before Doctor (see help doctor). Wiki detail on herbs and healing: Doctor & herbs.

Dunkrist

Hardiys Hojlen in Dunkrist (speaks Drow) teaches craft skills. From in-game list:

Skill Cost to learn Abilities required
Forging 1,440 None
Mining 1,200 None

The teacher in Dunkrist teaches the following (from in-game list):

Skill Cost to learn Abilities required
Teaching 12 None

The Natural Healer in Dunkrist (speaks Drow) teaches the following (from in-game list):

Skill Cost to learn Abilities required
First aid 1,380 None
Harvesting 600 None

These are lower-tier healing skills on the path to Doctor (see also Dunagin’s Herbalist trainer above).

Deepwater

Deepwater has one Professions Trainer (speaks Drow). From in-game list or list professions:

Profession Cost to learn Abilities required
Archer 60,000 Dexterity 15
Bladesman 60,000 Dexterity 14
Blind-fighter 40,000 Dexterity 14
Brawler 15,000 Strength 14 and Dexterity 12
Duelist 60,000 Dexterity 12 and Strength 12
Sapper 55,000 Strength 13 and Dexterity 11
Tactician 25,000 Intelligence 11 and Strength 11

Tiers: Brawler and Tactician are lower professions; Archer, Bladesman, Blind-fighter, Duelist, and Sapper are upper — each needs the matching lower path first (see individual help topics).
