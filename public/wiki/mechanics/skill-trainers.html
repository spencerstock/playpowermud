Skill trainers

Some NPCs teach individual skills (forging, mining, climbing, languages, and similar) outside your guild. They are not professions trainers and not your guild trainer for combat skills. At a skill teacher, use list for prices, then learn <skill> when you have enough drakaan. Guild training (train all, cost) is covered in the beginner's guide — Training skills.

Costs below are in drakaan from in-game list output. Add more sections as players map surface cities and other Underdark towns.

Underdark (UD)

Dunkrist — Hardiys Hojlen

Hardiys Hojlen (speaks Drow) teaches craft skills in the Duergar city of Dunkrist. He is in the room above the shopkeeper in the merchant plaza (the general shop east of the plaza centre).

Skill Cost to learn Abilities required
Forging 1,440 None
Mining 1,200 None

Example: list then learn forging or learn mining. Travel notes for Dunkrist: Underdark directions.
