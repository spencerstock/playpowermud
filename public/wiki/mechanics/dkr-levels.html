DKR levels

DKR (Dragon Killer Rating) is Powermud’s dragon-hunting prestige track. Only the character who lands the killing blow on a dragon gets credit toward huge kills and DKR progress — assists do not count. In-game: help dragon_guide (section 8), help dkr, help dragonfear.

What DKR does

From help dragon_guide (paraphrased):

  • DKR rises when you kill dragons yourself; stronger (higher-level) dragons raise DKR faster.
  • Higher DKR can mean less clerical/magical spell misfires and less susceptibility to dragonfear (gear and race resistances still apply separately; see item resistances).
  • Each dragon kill also adds one huge kill for that character (same killing-blow rule).

How to check your DKR
