Armor resize

Some smiths resize armor so it fits a different body size (for another race or an alt). Only pieces that have never been resized before are eligible — resizing twice weakens the material. Use lower case for all commands.

Commands

  • price <armor> to <size> — quote before you pay (Harius in Kalinport).
  • resize <armor> to <size> — perform the resize in the smith's room.
  • ask <npc> about resizing — full rules from the NPC. Generic ask about resizing may fail; name the smith (for example ask harius about resizing).

Sizes and races

From Harius in Kalinport (ask harius about resizing):

Size Races
Tiny Halfling, Gnome
Small Dwarf, Duergar
Medium Elf, Drow
Regular Human, Half-Elf, Half-Orc
Large Half-Ogre

Kalinport — Harius

In the Merchant's Diamond: a travelling Smith has a forge shop (room text mentions a forge, cooling trench, and a back door). The smith in the front room buys and repairs; resizing is in the back room.

  • From the street: enter the smithy (east into the shop in recorded walks).
  • door — open workroom with an anvil and material shelves; room text says armor can be resized here.
  • Harius (common) — l at harius notes he resizes armor; ask him about resizing for the command list.

Exits: front room west (street) and door (back); back room door to the main smithy. Collin Grokker's smithy elsewhere in Kalinport is for buying gear and repairs — see the beginner's guide.
