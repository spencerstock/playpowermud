Dragon Killer Rating (DKR)

DKR is the dragon-killing prestige title on character (DKR : …). Killing blows on dragons raise DKR and huge kills; assists do not count. Higher DKR can reduce spell misfires and dragonfear susceptibility (gear and race resistances stack separately; see item resistances).
