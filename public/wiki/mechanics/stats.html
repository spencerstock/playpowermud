Dragon killer ranks

These labels are the dragon killer ranks. Each step reduces your vulnerability to dragonfear (gear and race resistances stack separately; see item resistances). You move up a rank by landing the killing blow on a dragon; assists alone do not count.

Rank names, in order:

neophyte, journeyman, expert, adept, master, grand master, virtuoso, then virtuoso followed by doa, tre, patru, cinc, sasi, shapte, opt, noh, zeche.
