Daily tips

Short advice from veteran players Petrus and Frougus, taken from tells and shouts in the current wipe. These are community notes, not official help — check in game before you bet a run on them. More beginner coverage lives on the Beginner's guide.

Goals and habits

  • Have a goal, even short term. — Petrus (tell)

Loot and corpses

  • Check for magic items on a corpse before you leave the room. Too many good drops from guards get left on the ground. — Petrus (tell)
  • Guards can drop random low-level magic. Petrus was only seeing that pattern from guards, not every mob. — Petrus (tell)

Gear and weapons

  • The Rimer is a terrific weapon. — Frougus (judge / tell)
  • Giants at Orrowen Keep carry a good blunt weapon (worth a look if you want blunt avoidance or a blunt main). — Petrus (shout)

Guilds and travel

  • Disciples of Xenei start in their guild hometown and leave when they are able. — Petrus (tell)
  • Thieves Guild (Deepwater): outside the guild building. — Frougus (tell)

Combat and dragons

  • Brass dragons entangle — watch for it. — Frougus (shout); see also Combat.
  • Dragons can be in the area before you're ready. “Too early for dragons” is a fair warning when you're still building. — Frougus (shout)
  • There may be a dragon around a hunting spot — ask or scout before you commit. — Frougus (shout)

Build notes (shout chat)

  • Bard troubadour on Threshold Militia felt very grindy; some songs did not stack cleanly with gear. — Frougus (shout)
  • High charisma builds trade stats for haggle. Frougus was unsure which guild fit best at 12 charisma when several classes could learn the same skill. — Frougus (shout)
Tips are copied from player chat; wording is kept close to what was said. If you have a correction or another tip from Petrus or Frougus, open an issue or pull request on the Play Powermud repo.
